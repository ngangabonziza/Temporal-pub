# Temporal-pub

Public files referenced by dashboards.

## mh_dashboard_maps

District maps of epilepsy, schizophrenia and depression: all cases (new + old) reported on the HMIS diagnosis lines in June 2026 by facilities of the health centre level (health centres, medicalized health centres, prison clinics, health posts). District-level aggregate counts only. Source: HMIS. Dashboard-sized copies (1,800 pixels wide) of the 600 dpi originals.
